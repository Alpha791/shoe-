CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Customer rights & privacy notice

We want customers to understand what happens when they browse the FRIGATES Shoe Store, add items to a cart or contact us to place an order.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Privacy notice

Who is responsible?

FRIGATES Stores operates this online shoe catalogue. For privacy questions, contact alpha39frigates@gmail.com or WhatsApp +254 717 616 895.

Information this website handles

  • Shopping cart: Items and quantities you add are stored in your browser's local storage so the cart can remain available on that browser. The cart is not sent to a FRIGATES order database by this static website.
  • Product browsing: The catalogue can be searched and filtered in your browser. The site does not ask you to create an account.
  • When you contact us: If you choose to open WhatsApp or email us, information you decide to send may include your name, phone number, selected products, sizes, delivery details and message content.
  • Hosting and technical data: GitHub Pages hosts this website. The hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, browser information and access logs under its own terms and privacy practices.

Why information may be used

Information you send to us is used to respond to enquiries, confirm product availability, sizes, prices and delivery arrangements, and handle a requested order. We do not use this static storefront to take card details or process online payments.

Sharing and external services

When you follow a WhatsApp link, you leave this website and interact with WhatsApp/Meta. Any information you submit there is handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy. GitHub Pages provides website hosting. We do not sell customer personal information. We do not control the privacy practices of external providers.

Retention and security

Cart data remains in your browser until you clear it or remove the site's stored data. Messages and order information sent to FRIGATES may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, fulfil and document the enquiry or transaction, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children

This store is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has sent personal information that should be addressed.

2. Your privacy rights

Where applicable under the laws of Kenya, including the Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your personal data is used.
  • Request access to personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Request deletion of personal data, subject to lawful retention requirements.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing, where the law provides that right.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out.
  • Raise a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

To make a request, contact alpha39frigates@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. Tell us what you are asking for and how we can identify the relevant conversation or transaction. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will handle requests within the time required by applicable law and explain if a lawful exception applies.

You can also clear your local shopping cart using the cart controls or remove this site's stored data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not delete messages already sent through WhatsApp or information held by an external provider.

3. Store information and ordering terms

  • Product listings, images, descriptions and displayed prices are provided for shopping information and may change. An item shown online is not a guarantee of current stock.
  • Sizes, availability, final price, delivery options, delivery fees and fulfilment timing must be confirmed directly with FRIGATES before an order is accepted.
  • The WhatsApp checkout creates a message for you to review and send. It does not itself complete a purchase, reserve stock, charge a payment method or confirm an order.
  • Do not send card numbers, PINs, passwords or other payment credentials in a WhatsApp message.
  • Any applicable consumer rights under Kenyan law remain unaffected by this notice.
